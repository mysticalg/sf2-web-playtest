The standing reference moves from the trim pixels around `(35,23)`/`(42,24)` to visor pixels `(37,22)`/`(40,23)`; the corresponding translated poses use the same correction. Every other character's eye data, including Sagat's, is unchanged.

Changing collision-box centres, facing and subpixel actor movement cannot move that registration within the sprite. Original falling/lying mouth landmarks remain available. Hidden or uncertain faces receive no guessed mark; this is not a claim that every visible face in every animation has been individually reviewed. The original skin mask still protects hair, clothes and equipment.

## Choke Hold

Abigail retains his five existing specials and gains **Choke Hold** on **HP + LK** or **half-circle forward + punch**. The eighth startup tick checks for a living grounded victim within 64 pixels. Original grip/lift frames **19/21** animate the hold and use the existing source hand anchors. A missed or airborne target recovers without damage.

The held player changes directions and taps attacks to fill the existing **BREAK FREE** meter. Fresh attack presses contribute two points; a new non-neutral direction contributes one, capped at three points per tick. Fourteen points releases the victim. A held button does not repeatedly add escape points. The hold inflicts four damage after 20 ticks, then every 30 ticks, and releases after at most 180 ticks. Interruption, either fighter's KO and round reset release the attachment.

When selected as a death move, the first actual damaging choke contact is its final impact. The attachment releases in that same tick. After the short final-contact pause, the winner enters the victory pose without clutching the hidden victim. Andore's selected finishing choke uses the same final-contact release; his ordinary nonlethal carry, jump and slam controls remain intact. The finisher adapter's automatic hold-to-jump input is scoped to Andore.

## Guest fire reactions

Psycho Crusher uses native reaction **34** (blue/psycho burn), and ordinary flame uses native reaction **24** (orange burn). AI guests struck in the air retain the burn type from the real original attack record and run the existing native burn fall from their current height. This is an AI guest extension; full original airborne-controller parity is not claimed. Stock WW/CE reaction admission remains unchanged.

The shared native burn artwork renders while burning. Subsequent fall, lying, get-up and dizzy aliases render the guest's own original poses, instead of exposing the stock Balrog body used by the underlying source-state table. The procedural pale flame overlay is bypassed on this path.
