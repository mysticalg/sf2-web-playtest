# Abigail authored Spinning Piledriver artwork

The original 27 Abigail crops retain their pixels, size and origins. The supplied original pose package has standing/run/rage/punch/grab/squat/lift/fall artwork, but no complete seated turn for a spinning piledriver. Five extra seated views are generated SF2 adaptation artwork, not recovered Capcom animation frames. Native grip19, squat20 and lift21 remain the preparation; native recovery26 and idle4 remain the return.

The appended views are frame27 (front quarter), 28 (back), 29 (side), 30 (rear quarter), and31 (front). Every view is92pixels high, matching the original crouch20, with its sole baseline at+3. Abigail's actor scale remains1; original idle height109 and all six walking poses remain unchanged. The original1024x327atlas region retains all1,339,392RGBA channels. Original crouching-fist RGBA SHA256 remains `c2104eedeabd34f76f4a5dc48de4a7bc53fd86228fdb02702e168c87b7094e8a`.

Generated output was sampled with nearest-neighbour to native crouch height92, quantized to the15RGB colours used by the original normal poses, and given binary transparency. No original frame was resized. The two grip refinements replace only frame27's distal arm rectangle(43,29)..(69,55) and frame28's far-arm rectangle(60,20)..(81,54). All26,736/26,952channels outside those rectangles remain identical to the accepted generated views. The rear far forearm points forward behind the torso; its hand is occluded by the body instead of floating at the outside edge.

The full turn uses frame27,29,30,28,mirrored30,mirrored29,mirrored27,31, at4ticks per step. The frontal view closes the return through the front. Victim source hit pose22 is used through the turn and original hit pose20 on the frontal return, keeping the upright waist between the hands. These are original victim pixels; neither victim nor attacker is rescaled. The inverted head stays under the groin atY12 during the turn and the opaque crown meets the existing native sole/floor plane at impact.

The authoring proof packet is preserved in the isolated checkout under `work/abigail-integration/`: `grip-final.png/json` labels all16grip anchors across the eight steps against original Ryu waist/leg pixels (0..2.83pixels from the contact centres, inside the four-pixel hand radius); `full-360.gif` shows the complete cycle; `native-scale-comparison.png` compares original idle/crouch and all five new views at native1x. Vertical jump travel is removed only from the turn review so every pose can be compared. The actual controller separately reaches203pixels of jump height. Source/import/palette/crop hashes and protected-region counts are recorded in `spin-import-proof.json`, `grip-refinement-proof.json`, `art-proof.json` and `normal-results.json`.
