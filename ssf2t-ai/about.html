Original Super Turbo, in the browser

This preview runs the original European game's fighters, CPU opponents, moves and stages as static native code. Open your own ssf2t.zip locally to play; your ROM is never uploaded.

All sixteen standard fighters and Akuma have passed browser selection and battle checks. Old forms retain their original hidden codes. Difficulty and speed use the original game's settings.

Complete Ryu, Ken and Cammy arcade clears, original endings, staff roll, Continue and Game Over are verified. This update also recovers native Balrog/Vega victory animations and Honda defeat screens, verified from ordinary inputs against a full offline instruction oracle for both CPUs. Dhalsim, Cammy and T. Hawk special-input stops have also been repaired and checked against an independent offline instruction oracle. Other complete playthroughs and mechanics are still being tested. Audio plays the original 1.04 sound program through a reference mixer, with some spatial DSP features absent. This is a verified playable milestone; full native-game verification is ongoing.

Optional AI blood effects use original head colliders, confirmed health loss and original camera coordinates. Stronger hits and specials spray more blood; living fighters at 20% health or less drip from the face. Floor stains persist for the round and follow the native camera. Blocked chip emits no hit blood. The switch disables and clears the presentation effects without changing native game state. Final Fight fighters, possessed variants and death combos are not yet integrated into this native route.
