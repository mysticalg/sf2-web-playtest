Original Super Turbo, in the browser

This preview runs the original European game's fighters, CPU opponents, moves and stages as static native code. Open your own ssf2t.zip locally to play; your ROM is never uploaded.

All sixteen standard fighters and Akuma have passed browser selection and battle checks. Old forms retain their original hidden codes. Difficulty and speed use the original game's settings.

A complete Ryu arcade clear, original ending, staff roll, Continue and Game Over are verified. This update also recovers native Balrog/Vega victory animations and Honda defeat screens, verified from ordinary inputs against a full offline instruction oracle for both CPUs. Other endings and some mechanics are still being tested. Audio plays the original 1.04 sound program through a reference mixer, with some spatial DSP features absent. This is a verified playable milestone; full native-game verification is ongoing.
