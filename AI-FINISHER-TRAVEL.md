# Travelling AI finishers

Normal finishing attacks now use physical up/back impulses and gravity, with natural floor landings and another jump to chase an airborne opponent. Attack admission forecasts the actual source animation's first active frame. A genuine whiff finishes its animation before a bounded retry; it never adds a hit. Direction edges retain crouch → stand → air → stand → crouch control. Specials retain their own physical controllers and complete their full animation before the next action.

The combo rotation includes offensive specials, and the final action remains the requested special. Multi-hit actions count every genuine accepted contact; the diagnostic target grows with those additional contacts. Ordinary contacts hold for ten physical ticks, limiting even successive windows of one move to six contacts per second. Rare late-combo contacts hold for three ticks, allowing up to twenty per second. These are maximum rates; complete source moves can take longer.

After the first contact the combat scene travels at 420/90 pixels per physical tick, following the attacker's facing. The original stage tile maps repeat. Active movement anchors and projectiles translate with the travelling fight, while afterimages remain at fixed logical map coordinates. Trails still emit at most six times per second, become opaque black silhouettes and stop/clear at the final contact.

The CE adapter keeps its native object coordinates near the source's legal range by rebasing the physical scene. A separate logical offset keeps the stage, trail positions and camera continuous. Existing blood and loose weapon positions rebase together; live weapon flights and wall-move anchors remain coherent. Round reset clears this origin.
